History is a starting point, not a costume.

Ancient mosaics, everyday printed matter, an expressive canine silhouette: we look for the logic that gives an image its force. The aim is not to reproduce a period, but to find something that can do a new job.

QUESTIONS TO KEEP ON THE TABLE
— What makes the silhouette legible?
— Which details carry the character?
— What survives a change of scale?
