PROJECT ENQUIRY / A GUIDE, NOT A TEST

A few useful
starting points.

Use these prompts to tell us about your project by email. Share what you know; we can work through the open questions together.

01 / YOUR NAME & ORGANISATION

Who are you, and who are you getting in touch on behalf of?

02 / YOUR EMAIL

Include the best email address for the conversation.

03 / WHAT ARE YOU THINKING?

Identity & systems · Editorial & print
Digital & motion · Image & material
Something else / not sure

You don’t need to choose a discipline yet. The right medium can be part of the conversation.

04 / A FEW WORDS ABOUT THE PROJECT

What are you making, and why? Who is it for? What already exists, and what is still open? Start with the question, not a polished pitch.

05 / TIMELINE — OPTIONAL

A launch date, a deadline or simply “still open”. Let us know if anything is fixed.

06 / BUDGET — OPTIONAL

An approximate range helps frame the scope. If you’re unsure, we can discuss it.

07 / SUPPORTING CONTEXT — OPTIONAL

A link to a brief, reference, existing work or rough scope. A few honest questions are just as useful.

NO PERFECT BRIEF REQUIRED. SEND WHAT YOU HAVE.
