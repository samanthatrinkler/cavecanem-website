PROJECT C / A—C
PiqPiq —
WhiteFrontier

C / IDENTITY / ILLUSTRATION / PACKAGING

COMPETITION ENTRY / 2024

A collaborative identity for WhiteFrontier Brewery’s label design contest. An illustrated creature, oversized red lettering and electric green, yellow and red gradient turn the can into a compact, irreverent graphic surface. A competition entry for WhiteFrontier brewery—balancing spice, lightness and the joy of being together. This beer embodies the spirit of defiance, the strength to dare, and the passion of a burning desire; the desire to live with enthusiasm and determination.

01 / PIQPIQ FOR WHITEFRONTIER — COMPETITION ENTRY, 2024. ORIGINAL PROJECT ARTWORK.

[01] / A COLLABORATIVE IDENTITY

A small format.
A loud personality.

01 / THE CONCEPT

Discover an amber chili-infused beer—a vibrant, bold elixir full of zest, crafted for those who seek to push their own boundaries and live every moment with intensity.

02 / THE LETTERING

Large red letters change direction across the cylindrical surface. The typography joins the illustration rather than sitting apart from it.

03 / THE SURFACE

Green and yellow make a high-energy field. The paired views reveal how the same visual language travels around the can.

Drawn to stand out.

Illustration, type and colour share the same space. Explore the two views of the supplied can design.
